## Users

General/mixed prospective clients evaluating whether to hire Jeffrey for a website, funnel, CRM setup, or automation project. Not narrowed to one niche (e.g. not exclusively coaches or agencies).

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio website for Jeffrey Brian Castro to showcase his work building websites, funnels, CRM systems, and automation workflows, and to convert visitors into inbound client inquiries/booked calls.

## Positioning

Combines hands-on delivery across the full client-ops stack (site/funnel build + CRM setup + backend automation) rather than a single specialty — built and shipped using Claude Code as part of the workflow.

## Operating Context

Primary tools used in delivery: GoHighLevel (GHL), n8n, Zapier, Make, and Claude Code.

Service areas: Websites, Sales Funnels, CRM Setup (GHL), Automation Workflows.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static site, no backend/CMS. Content (projects, testimonials, credentials, contact links) is not yet finalized.

## Evidence on Hand

None confirmed yet. User explicitly requested a skeleton now with placeholder content for projects, testimonials, credentials, and contact links, to be swapped in later. All such content must be clearly structured as placeholder, not fabricated as real claims (no invented client names, quotes, or stats).

## Product Principles

- Lead with breadth across the client-ops stack (site + funnel + CRM + automation), not a single-service pitch.
- Keep every placeholder obviously a placeholder in structure/wording so nothing invented reads as a real claim.
- Structure content so real projects/testimonials/credentials can drop in later without a redesign.
- Static, zero-build-step delivery that deploys anywhere.
