Common questions

Things people ask before they book.

What does mercury-safe actually mean?

We follow the IAOMT SMART protocol on every amalgam removal: high-volume suction, dam isolation, supplemental oxygen, and dedicated air filtration. The result is significantly lower mercury vapor exposure during the removal compared to standard practice. We also do not place new amalgam fillings.

Are you in-network with my insurance?

We're in-network with Delta Dental PPO, Cigna PPO, Anthem Blue Cross, Aetna PPO, and most BlueShield plans. Use the insurance checker above for a rough estimate. Front desk verifies actual coverage before your visit.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

The first cleaning + exam is flat $215. We also offer a Westmont Membership Plan: $39 per month covers two cleanings, exams, X-rays, and 15 percent off everything else. Cancel anytime.

Do you take kids?

Yes. We see kids from age 2 for a five-minute "happy visit" - count the teeth, get a sticker. First real cleaning around age 4. We screen for tongue tie, lip tie, and airway issues at every pediatric visit.

What's the airway / sleep screening about?

Sleep apnea, snoring, and grinding often show up in the mouth before they show up in a sleep study. We screen at every adult and pediatric visit, and refer to a sleep physician when indicated. Oral appliance therapy is available if surgery is not the right fit.

Can I see a treatment plan first?

Yes. After the first exam we email a written plan with each item priced, what your insurance is expected to cover, and your share. No work starts until you sign that plan back to us.
