Six workflows, one booking pipeline

Built in GoHighLevel. Published and running.

Every stage of a booking has a workflow behind it. An enquiry from Messenger or the site form becomes an opportunity with an owner on it; from there the pipeline moves itself — booked, attended, no-show, repeat or lost — and each move fires the follow-up that stage needs.

Enquiry in

Messenger reply or site form

Booked

Confirmed, then reminded twice

Attended

Thanked, stage moves itself

Repeat client

Promo a week later

No show

Chased for a rebooking

Closed lost

Three emails over two months

  1. 001

    New enquiry intake

    A Messenger reply or a form submission tags the contact, finds or creates the opportunity, assigns an owner and notifies the team. An hour later it checks whether a booking happened and flags the ones that did not.

  2. 002

    Appointment booked

    Confirmation email the moment the slot is taken, a reminder an hour later, and a final one after that.

  3. 003

    Attended

    The team gets the notification, the client gets a thank-you, and the deal moves itself to Closed / Repeat.

  4. 004

    No show

    A no-show email goes out, then the workflow checks for a rebooking after an hour and again two days later. Two days after that with still no booking, a last email goes out and the deal moves to Closed Lost.

  5. 005

    Closed / repeat

    Tagged as a repeat client, then a promo email seven days later.

  6. 006

    Closed lost

    The no-show tag comes off and three re-engagement emails go out — two weeks on, twenty days after that, then thirty more.
