Technology that earns its keep

Less guesswork, fewer visits, and zero goopy impressions.

We invested in the equipment that actually shortens your treatment - not the equipment that looks impressive in a brochure.

  • iTero 5D digital scanner

    Full bite map in two minutes - no putty trays, no gagging, and you see the projected outcome on day one.

  • Low-dose 3D cone-beam imaging

    One-tenth the radiation of a standard pano, with 3x the diagnostic detail for jaw and root positioning.

  • Self-ligating Damon brackets

    No elastic ties, fewer hygiene issues, and most patients move through treatment 3 to 6 months faster than with conventional braces.

  • AI-assisted aligner planning

    Algorithm flags root collisions and over-correction risks before fabrication - so refinements drop by half.

  • Same-day in-house retainers

    3D printed and ready within four hours - no overnight courier, no living without one for a week.

  • Patient-side progress portal

    Track aligner trays, see your projected finish week, and message the office without leaving a voicemail.

  • Soft-tissue diode laser

    Gentle gum reshaping and frenectomies in office, with no scalpel and same-day recovery for most patients.

Parent FAQ

The questions we answer most.

If yours isn't here, text or call us. We answer questions even before booking - and the consult itself is always free.

What's the best age to start orthodontic care?
The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a first check at age 7. That doesn't mean braces at 7 - it means we look at jaw growth and incoming teeth so we can catch real problems early and skip non-issues entirely. Most kids start active treatment between 11 and 14.
How long does treatment usually take?
Most adult Invisalign cases finish in 9 to 14 months. Full traditional braces for teens average 18 to 24 months. Complex bites with jaw alignment can run longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline at your free consult, not a vague range.
Do you accept insurance and offer payment plans?
We accept Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, BCBS, and most major plans. Beyond that, we offer 0% in-house financing for up to 36 months with no credit check, and we apply HSA / FSA dollars without making you chase paperwork.
Can my teen still play sports with braces?
Yes - we make a custom-fit mouthguard included in every braces plan, and they're cleared for football, lacrosse, soccer, and contact sports. For Invisalign Teen, the aligners come out for games and go right back in afterward.
What happens after braces or Invisalign come off?
Retainers, every time. We provide one set of clear retainers and one fixed lower retainer included in every comprehensive plan, plus 24 months of complimentary fit checks. Skipping retainers is the single biggest reason orthodontic work relapses.
What should I expect at the first visit?
A 60-minute consult: full digital scan, low-dose 3D x-ray, sit-down with Dr. Velasquez to walk through findings, and a printed plan with timeline and pricing options before you leave. No high-pressure pitch, no commitment, and no charge.
