- Concept 03

A cosmopolitan answer to Manila's vertical century.

Solana sits at the meeting of Bonifacio High Street and 32nd Avenue, where BGC's new northern skyline becomes a horizon you can walk into. The brief was simple. Build a residence that belongs to a global capital, but speaks Tagalog at the threshold.

Sustainability is structural. A photovoltaic facade quietly powers the public floors. Vertical gardens span six interventions across the tower, cooling units passively and softening the figure of the building from the street. Rainwater capture serves the landscaped amenities deck on level 31.

The result reads as one gesture. Architecture, interior detail, and the curated landscape are designed by a single studio under a single thesis. Spaces that hold light, breath, and the long view.
