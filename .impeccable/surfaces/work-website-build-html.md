THESIS: A case study here proves the single-operator claim by drawing the delivered system, not by narrating a testimonial. The page owns one idea — this is the machine that got built, and one person wired every box in it. It refuses the agency case-study default: a hero photo of a laptop, three paragraphs of process prose, and a metric the reader cannot trace to anything.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited whole from index.html and DESIGN.md — lavender paper ground (#F6F4FB), near-black navy ink (#14121F), one amber accent (#FF6B2C) reserved for action and live-state, white hairline-bordered cards at 24px radius, Figtree for every word, Space Mono for numeric proof only, flat at rest and lifting only on hover. No new tokens, no second identity. The one new component family is the system diagram: hairline-stroked SVG nodes with mono labels, drawn in ink with amber marking only the live path.

STORY: The visitor arrives from the homepage Projects card already believing breadth; this page has to convert breadth into credibility. They read what kind of engagement this was, see the finished system drawn as one diagram they can follow end to end, understand the problem it replaced, scan what was actually built and on which tools, reach the outcome, and leave through a contact action or into the next case study.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sidebar rail unchanged on the left. The primary contact action sits at the page's close, not in the first viewport; the diagram is what earns the scroll.
